Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Island Money is a small personal project that shows your balance in the look of the Dynamic Island desktop widget. This page says what it does with your data. It is written in plain words on purpose.

What the app stores

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time code. There are no passwords.
  • Your balance and transactions: typed in by you, or read from your bank if you connect it.
  • Bank connection details: the name of your bank, the identifier of the consent given to Enable Banking and its end date, the identifier and the last four characters of the IBAN of each account. Never your bank login, password or card numbers: you sign in on your bank's own page and the app never sees those.
  • Cards you add on the Pay tab: the bank, brand, last four digits, a name and the background you choose (a small photo if you pick one). Never the full card number, the expiry date or the security code.
  • On your device: the last balance shown (so the app opens fast), your theme and palette choice, and your sign-in session. You can erase them by signing out or clearing the site data.

Who handles it

  • Supabase hosts the database and the sign-in. The data is protected so that each user can only read their own rows.
  • Enable Banking is the regulated service (open banking, PSD2) that reads your bank account with your consent, only if you connect a bank. Their policy applies to what they do.
  • GitHub Pages serves the web page, and jsDelivr serves one script library; like any web server they see your IP address.

Your data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

What it is used for

Only to show you your own balance and history in the app and in the Dynamic Island widget on your computer.

Your choices

  • Disconnect your bank in Settings: its accounts and transactions are deleted from the app, and the consent is cancelled at Enable Banking.
  • Withdraw the consent at any time in your bank's own app, which stops all access to your account.
  • Ask for your data to be exported or deleted through the contact below.

How long it is kept

Until you disconnect the bank or ask for the account to be deleted. Bank access also ends on its own when the consent runs out (usually 90 to 180 days) unless you renew it.

Contact

Open an issue on github.com/DorianRecht-WS/island-money and say that you have a privacy question, without writing personal details there (issues are public). A private way to reach the maintainer will be given in reply.
