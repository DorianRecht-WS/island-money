Island Money is a free, non-commercial personal project. By using it you agree to the following.

What it is

A tool that shows a balance and a short history, typed in by you or read from your own bank account through Enable Banking. It is for your personal use. It does not move money, make payments or give financial advice.

Your account

  • You sign in with your email and a one-time code. Keep your email account secure; whoever can read your email can sign in.
  • Only connect bank accounts that belong to you.
  • You can stop at any time: disconnect your bank in Settings and stop using the app.

Accuracy

Numbers come from your bank and can be late, incomplete or wrong (for example pending operations are not included). Do not rely on this app for decisions that matter: check your bank's own app.
